The founder

Hi, I’m Alicia

The founder and heart behind Nettoyage Nordique MTL.

What started as a simple idea has grown into something that means so much more to me than I ever expected. Building this business has taken hard work, long days and nights, a lot of learning, and most importantly, trusting myself enough to go for it.

Every new client, every recommendation, every message and every person who chooses to trust me with their home or commercial space reminds me why I started. There’s something incredibly special about watching something that was once just an idea slowly become something real.

Nettoyage Nordique MTL isn’t just about cleaning. It’s about creating homes and commercial spaces that feel cleaner, fresher, lighter and more welcoming, all while providing reliable, professional service that people can count on.

To everyone who has supported me, booked with me, recommended me or simply cheered me on: thank you. You’ve played a part in this journey more than you know.

I’m so proud of how far we’ve come and even more excited for everything that’s still ahead.
